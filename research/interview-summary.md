# Interview Summary

**Method:** 1-on-1 interviews with 10 Splitwise users (college students to salaried and married professionals), about how they split group bills, track spending and settle up. Three interviews are summarised below; the other seven gave very similar answers. Names are removed.

## P1: 23, salaried
- Splitting is a hassle whenever he pays first.
- Feels uncomfortable asking friends for the money they owe.
- Pays from another app, forgets, and ends up paying late.
- Wants one place to log all his payments and track monthly expenses.
- Would like a trip planner where a group can set a shared budget.
- Wants to invest but has no time to learn; would like suggestions.

## P2: 17, college student
- Has no idea how much he spends or where; wants category-wise totals.
- Finds it uncomfortable to add friends' bills by hand and to ask for money back.
- Wants to learn money management and start saving; would like tips on what to cut.

## P3: married, salaried
- Wants daily expense tracking and a monthly report.
- Finds it hard to enter long bills by hand.
- Wants to invest but lacks time.
- Forgets to pay his Splitwise dues and feels embarrassed when someone asks.

## Patterns

| Pain point | Detailed interviews | Covered today? |
|---|---|---|
| Entering long bills by hand | 3 of 3 | Splitwise Pro: receipt scanning and itemization |
| No category-wise or monthly spending view | 3 of 3 | Splitwise Pro: charts and graphs |
| Awkward to ask for money, or to be asked | 3 of 3 | Only a manual Remind button |
| Forgetting to pay because paying happens in another app | 2 of 3 | No UPI payment inside Splitwise |

**Insight:** the unsolved problems are social, not technical. People avoid asking, and people who owe forget and then feel embarrassed.
